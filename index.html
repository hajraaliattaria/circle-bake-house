Our Story

A new experience for Sahiwal.

At Circles , we believe every city deserves a place that feels fresh, modern, and full of flavor, and that is exactly why we brought something new to Sahiwal. In major cities, people enjoy more than just bakeries — they enjoy a full experience. A stylish place where you can sip on a cold pressed juice, enjoy a freshly made sandwich, or celebrate life's sweet moments with a beautiful cake. But Sahiwal, with all its charm and culture, had been waiting for something like this. So we created Circles — the city's first modern bakery café.

From rich, buttery croissants and gourmet pizzas to creamy chillers, fresh salads, indulgent brownies, and live-made sandwiches and paninis, everything at Circles is made to delight. We use quality ingredients, creative recipes, and offer a relaxing space so you get more than just food. You get a moment to enjoy, share, and remember. Our products are premium, but we have kept something for everyone — whether you are treating yourself, celebrating with friends, or just grabbing a quick bite. Circles is more than a bakery. A new experience for Sahiwal.
